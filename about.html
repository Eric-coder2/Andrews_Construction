About Us

Andrews Construction LLC was founded in 2018 by Andres, a licensed contractor with over 15 years of experience in the trades. Before starting his own company, Andres spent years learning every aspect of residential renovation since 2010.

Today, Andres handles most of the work himself, occasionally bringing along trusted helpers for larger jobs. Andrews Construction takes on projects of all sizes: from single-room refreshes to full kitchen and bathroom renovations. The approach is straightforward: listen to what the client wants, be flexible when plans change, and do not consider the job finished until the work meets the standard it was built to.

Outside of the job site, Andres is a husband and father of five, living in the Lothian, Maryland community. Building a business that his family can be proud of is what drives the standard of every project he takes on.
