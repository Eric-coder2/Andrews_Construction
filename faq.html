Frequently asked Questions

About The Company

Is Andrews Construction LLC licensed?

Yes — we hold contractor's license LIC. #135889, valid in Maryland.

How long have you been in business?

Andrews Construction was founded in 2018. Andrew has been working in the trades since 2010.

What areas do you serve?

We serve residential clients throughout DC, Maryland, and Virginia — the full DMV area.

Getting an Estimate

How do I get an estimate?

Contact us through the form on our Contact page or call/text 240-603-0873. We will schedule a site visit and send a detailed written estimate by email within a few days.

Is the estimate free?

Estimates are free for most locations in our primary service area. For locations outside that area, a small estimate fee may apply.

What do I need to have ready before requesting an estimate?

The more specific you can be, the better. Project address, what you want done, and any photos or measurements you have are all helpful.

The Project

Do you handle the full project or just part of it?

We handle every phase–from demolition and rough work through finishing.

Can I make changes after the project starts?

We are flexible on scope modifications. Keep in mind that additions to the project will affect the timeline and the estimate.

How do you handle payment?

We accept Zelle, Credit card, and Check. Payment structure is outlined in the written estimate before work begins.

What is the general payment structure?

Our payment model typically consists of a 50% initial deposit to begin the project, with later installments throughout the end of finishing.

What happens if something goes wrong after the project is complete?

Contact us directly. We stand behind our work and will address any issues related to our installation.
